# Knowledge Maintenance Quality

## Why this matters

The benefit of a [Persistent Wiki](../concepts/Persistent%20Wiki.md) can be lost if provenance, conflicts, stale claims, and navigation are not maintained.

## What is known

The example source explicitly identifies those maintenance duties as necessary. [Source record](../sources/src-44190eefdbf1.md)
